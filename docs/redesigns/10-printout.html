The

ConeSortium

A joke of a repo around four real ROS 2 nodes, written for Formula Student driverless racing. A student-built car with nobody in it laps a track marked only by cones: blue on its left, yellow on its right. The track is whatever lies between them.

STEPDESCRIPTIONSTATUS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
01 RUSHFind the cones in the LiDAR point cloudOK
02 FUSEDMap them on lap 1, localise against them afterOK
03 STELLATIONGrow a centerline between them for the carOK
04 POSERShow the cones in RViz. Optional, not in the raceSKIPPED
