The ConeSortium

A joke of a repo around four real ROS 2 nodes, written for Formula Student driverless racing.

A student-built car with nobody in it laps a track marked only by cones. Blue on its left, yellow on its right. The Club, the codenames and the black bars are jokes; the nodes are real.

  1. 1 · cuCONERUSHFinds the cones.
  2. 2 · cuCONEFUSEDMaps them, then localises.
  3. 3 · cuCONESTELLATIONGrows the centerline.
  4. Retired · CONEPOSERShows them in RViz.
