Comrades, the cones do not drift. Neither shall we.

Four real ROS 2 nodes, written for Formula Student driverless racing, where a student-built car with nobody in it laps a track marked only by cones. Blue on the left, yellow on the right.

CONESORTIUM · 2024–2026

The plan, in four stages

1

cuCONERUSH

Detect. Point cloud in, cone markers out, on CUDA buffers the whole way: filter, RANSAC ground, voxel clusters, cone-size check.

Quota met in1.1 ms
2

cuCONEFUSED

Map. Lap 1 records every cone. From lap 2 the map is frozen and the cones correct the odometry's drift.

Full update1.3 ms
3

cuCONESTELLATION

Plan. Triangulate the cone map, grow a centerline through it. Four backends, one byte-identical answer.

Backends4
4

CONEPOSER

Retired with honours. One matrix multiply, kept to show the cones in RViz as a ground-truth reference.
