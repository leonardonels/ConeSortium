What this file is actually about

This is a goliardic repo. The Club, the codenames and the black bars are jokes.

The nodes are real. They were written for Formula Student driverless racing, where a student-built car with nobody in it laps a track marked only by cones.

The cones are the whole job: blue on the car's left, yellow on its right, and the track is whatever lies between them.

CHAIN OF CUSTODY · WHO HANDS WHAT TO WHOM

  1. 1 · cuCONERUSHFinds the cones in the LiDAR point cloud.
  2. 2 · cuCONEFUSEDMaps them on lap 1 and localises against them after that.
  3. 3 · cuCONESTELLATIONGrows a centerline between them for the car to follow.
  4. Happily Retired · CONEPOSEROptional. Puts the cones where you can see them in RViz or Foxglove, as a ground-truth reference.
