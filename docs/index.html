MEMBERS ONLY

The ConeSortium is a ROS 2 workspace for Formula Student driverless racing, where a student-built car with nobody in it laps a track marked only by cones. Blue on its left, yellow on its right. The track is whatever lies between them.

The cones, at least, are reliable. They stay where you put them. They do not drift. They are, frankly, the most dependable part of the entire pipeline.

Every member lives as a git submodule, so the society is mostly an opinionated list of links to repos that each individually claim to be the important one.

Member No. 1 · cuCONERUSHFounding member · In good standing
FOLIO 1

The detection front-end. Point cloud in, cone markers out: XYZ filter, RANSAC ground segmentation, voxel clustering and cone-size filtering, on CUDA buffers the entire way through.

From a leisurely 10 ms on an Orin Nano Super to a borderline-rude 1.1 ms on an RTX 4060 Mobile. Asking the CPU to help would be embarrassing.

maxHeight must stay above the ground plane at maximum range, or far cones are silently dropped and everyone spends an afternoon blaming the clustering.

Its README still ships a real crash log under a heading called TODO, which remains the most honest section in this entire workspace.

Member No. 2 · cuCONEFUSEDIn good standing
FOLIO 2

The clever one. EKF-SLAM that treats FAST-LIMO odometry as a purely relative motion source and the track's static cones as landmarks.

Lap 1 builds the map with the pose gain zeroed. From lap 2 the map is rigid and only the 3x3 pose block is updated, so the pose snaps onto the cone map and stops drifting.

The CUDA backend keeps P and x on the device for the whole run: about 15 ms on a CPU core becomes about 1.3 ms on an Orin Nano. USE_CUDA=OFF still builds the slow version, for debugging and for humility.

Its record notes a recent repair: the member once really believed in infinity, but the GPU did not. Now it is just a ten hiding behind an infinite name. The members have been assured that ten is infinite enough.

Member No. 3 · cuCONESTELLATIONNewly admitted
FOLIO 3

The newest member, and the one that finally does something with the cones instead of merely admiring them. It Delaunay-triangulates the cone map and grows a centerline, called without irony The Way, one midpoint at a time.

Four interchangeable backends produce byte-identical Ways and differ only in how much of your Orin they set on fire. The original CPU backend is kept purely to prove the others identical: too slow to deploy, too useful to delete.

The diagnostic log line now costs more than the search it is measuring.

Member No. 4 · CONEPOSERMember emeritus
FOLIO 4

The middle child. It does exactly one matrix multiply, Tglobal = Todom · Tmarker, and it does not pretend otherwise. No filtering, no fusion, no drama.

No longer races, but still attends in RViz and Foxglove as a ground-truth reference. Content with a CPU and modest expectations.

Service record: one commit, ever, called "first commit". Sometimes you just need the cones somewhere else.
